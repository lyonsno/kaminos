Reference and parameter provenance
Adapted from Implicit Position-Based Fluids, figures 11–14. The published block flop uses 250K particles, 2 passes and 1/240 s. Its exact box dimensions are unspecified.
Our unit cube has spacing 1/resolution, sample volume spacing³, pressure and surface support 2×spacing, and collision offset ½spacing. Box: 3×3×1.3 units. Gravity 9.2 is retained from Kaminos. Damping beta .0113 and alternate compliance .001 are inherited experimental settings, not identified as paper defaults. Six planar wall contributions use the retained product-union approximation; corner overlap is approximate. Surface coefficient starts at zero to isolate pressure. Four steps run per displayed frame; the simulated clock is authoritative. No emitter, recycling, velocity smoothing, vorticity or speed clipping. Particle marker size affects display only. This is a comparable reference scene, not a numerical reproduction or performance benchmark.
