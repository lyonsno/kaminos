# Polygonal Cat Reconstruction Admission

The exact FLUX source was reconstructed by SF3D and Trellis, then inspected
across matched six-view orbits. The selection predicate was frozen before
inspection: naturalistic feline coherence across views dominates visible
tessellation similarity.

Trellis wins under that predicate. It preserves a recognizable feline body,
four-limb organization, and coherent hidden-side morphology around the orbit.
SF3D preserves the pale palette and privileged-view face more closely, but its
hidden-view body swells and its rear-limb organization partially fuses or
disappears. This admits Trellis as the causal-return source; it does not claim
that Trellis is globally superior or that either reconstruction preserves the
authored carrier quantitatively.

The selected Trellis source is view `az180-el12`, bound in `selection.json`.
The first successor is one matched FLUX cell, not a prompt matrix: same prompt,
seed, model, quantization, resolution, steps, and guidance as the original cat
cell. Its question is whether reconstruction alone moves the naturalistic
polygonal-cat basin. Fanout follows only after this cell is visually inspected.
