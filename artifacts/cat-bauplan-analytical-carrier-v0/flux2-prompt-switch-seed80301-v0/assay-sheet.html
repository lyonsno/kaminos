3. Research interpretation

Source authority

All five cells preserve the folded hindquarter, rear support spacing, axial bridge, shoulder separation, head placement, and four-support organization. The authored low-frequency event is already a strong control surface.

Phenotype authority

Changing only cat, dog, or bear produces coherent local changes in tail, feet, distal supports, and stance. The named taxon can modify phenotype without erasing global source organization.

Surface commitment and ceiling

Completing an outer skin is not implied by structural completion. It is a separate prompt axis. This one source and seed do not establish multi-seed robustness, anatomical fidelity, 3D correspondence, or general surface reliability.
