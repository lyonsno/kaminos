# Multi-source sparse-guidance assay

This assay asks whether the sparse-structure CFG transition observed on source `0066` is a reusable creature-control law or a source-specific accident. It holds seed, steps, resolution, meshing, texture decode, cascade mode, sparse rescale/interval, and downstream Shape guidance fixed while applying sparse CFG `0.0`, `0.25`, and `0.5` to three materially different creature sources.

## Claim boundary

The first assay established that sparse-stage pressure can change gross topology while dense Shape pressure mainly changes local detail and surface congestion for one source. This assay tests source dependence and repeatability. The transition threshold remains provisional until all three source sections have exact effective route receipts and admitted multi-view witnesses.

The visual comparison may support a bounded control law: lower sparse guidance permits more model-prior invention, while higher sparse guidance preserves more source identity. It cannot establish that one fixed CFG value produces the same morphology regime for every source, seed, or prompt basin.

The visual read will score two axes independently. **Identity retention** means the source body axis, terminal orientation, broad contact layout, and dominant mass distribution remain recognizable. **Prior contribution** means Trellis introduces a materially different silhouette class, appendage vocabulary, segmentation rhythm, or whole-organism gestalt rather than merely roughening the supplied surface. A useful inspiration regime needs both; resemblance alone is tracing, while novelty without lineage is replacement.

## Evidence contract

- Nine Trellis generations: three sources by three sparse guidance strengths.
- One fixed seed and one exact effective route family.
- Four distinct Blender camera witnesses per generated GLB.
- Every contact-sheet cell binds its source job, camera route, PNG hash, and originating GLB hash.
- Missing, failed, duplicate, fallback, or route-divergent jobs fail artifact assembly.

## Visual disposition

Direct inspection of the assembled contact sheet and the ambiguous `0032` front witnesses supports the sparse-stage lever and rejects a universal fixed threshold.

### Repeated model-prior basin at CFG 0.0

All three materially different sources collapse toward the same elongated axial or stalk-like organism class at sparse CFG `0.0`. The witnesses are coherent enough to be useful mutations, but they do not preserve source identity. Their measured anisotropy agrees with the visual read: normalized PCA secondary axes are `0.059` for `0066`, `0.104` for `0032`, and `0.107` for `0087`. CFG `0.0` is therefore an extreme prior-first mutation operator, not a general inspiration setting.

### Source-relative invention at CFG 0.25

CFG `0.25` is the strongest observed inspiration setting for two of the three sources. Source `0066` becomes a compact multipart insectoid/mammalian mutant, and source `0087` becomes a low armored quadruped/insectoid mutant. Both retain lineage cues while changing whole-organism gestalt rather than merely roughening the source.

Source `0032` does not recover its radial crawler body at CFG `0.25`. It becomes a coherent tall, one-eyed, hook-limbed organism with a detached organ. This is a productive miss and a strong prior-contribution witness, but it abandons the source contact layout and dominant mass distribution.

### Recovery and fragmentation at CFG 0.5

Sources `0066` and `0087` recover source-relative low armored bodies at CFG `0.5`. Source `0032` instead separates into two disconnected creature fragments. Its sparse voxel count rises only from `356` at CFG `0.25` to `435` at CFG `0.5`, while the other sources reach `2261` and `2000` sparse voxels at CFG `0.5`. More conditioning pressure did not monotonically improve this source inside the tested range.

### Bounded control law

Sparse-structure CFG is a gross topology and organism-gestalt lever. Lower pressure exposes a repeatable elongated axial model-prior basin; intermediate pressure can produce coherent source-relative novelty; higher pressure can recover source identity. The useful transition threshold is source-dependent. It must be selected with silhouette, connectedness, contact-layout, and multi-view identity witnesses rather than hardcoded globally.

The next narrow probe is source `0032` at sparse CFG `0.75` and `1.0`. That probe asks whether its radial crawler identity recovers above `0.5` or whether the current conditioning source itself is hostile. It does not reopen the already established conclusion that sparse guidance controls gross topology.
