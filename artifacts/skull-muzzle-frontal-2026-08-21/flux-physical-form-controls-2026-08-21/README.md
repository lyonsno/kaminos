# FLUX Physical-Form Control Atlas

The preceding four-seed physical-model atlas separated three behaviors that
broad style labels had conflated: identity retention, surface-frequency
suppression, and thin facial structure. This atlas keeps a physical carrier and
an identity-preservation sentence fixed while varying one local representation
choice at a time.

Four prompt families cover continuous muzzle construction, eye representation,
attached coat-form organization, and adjacent physical style basins. Seeds
`81432` and `81433` form wave one; seeds `81434` and `81435` form wave two. The
exact source bytes and FLUX route remain fixed across all 64 cells.

Prompts do not specify camera, framing, pose, or projection. Those remain
conditioned by the source image. Every output is retained and must be inspected
directly before any reconstruction nomination.

Claim ceiling: the atlas may identify repeatable image-generation controls and
nominate exact reconstruction sources. It cannot establish improved geometry,
winding, collision suitability, or production admission.

basins for continued comparison; it does not yet choose a production cast.
