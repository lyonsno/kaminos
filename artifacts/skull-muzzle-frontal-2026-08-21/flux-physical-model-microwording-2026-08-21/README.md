# FLUX Physical-Model Micro-Wording Atlas

This atlas follows direct inspection of all 48 cells in the reconstruction
wording atlas. Four-seed recurrence showed that physical-material language
changes the source representation substantially:

- `polystone` most consistently turns coat texture into owned sculpted forms,
  but can produce unpainted eyes and thin whiskers;
- `silicone maquette` simplifies the envelope reliably, with the same eye and
  whisker risks;
- feature-animation language preserves identity but often leaves photoreal
  high-frequency fur;
- game and cel language can collapse into flat illustration unless the prompt
  explicitly establishes a physical three-dimensional model.

Sixteen short prompt interventions test those observed boundaries across four
fresh matched seeds. The exact source bytes and FLUX route remain fixed. The
prompts change representation and surface organization only; camera, framing,
projection, pose, and anatomy remain source-conditioned.

Wave 1 uses seeds 81427 and 81428. Wave 2 uses seeds 81429 and 81430. Every
cell is retained, including flat, over-detailed, anatomically shifted, or
otherwise unsuccessful outputs.

Claim ceiling: this atlas may identify repeatable physical-model wording and
nominate exact reconstruction sources. It cannot establish improved geometry
without matched TRELLIS reconstruction and close-orbit inspection.
