# FLUX Reconstruction Wording Atlas

This targeted atlas follows the broad representation atlas. It tests twelve
wording variants inside four successful style families across four matched
seeds, for 48 exact cells. The canonical source bytes, route, and image settings
remain fixed. Prompts change representation and surface organization only;
camera, framing, projection, pose, and anatomy remain source-conditioned.

The families are natural hybrid, physical maquette/collectible, cinematic/game
character, and graphic/low-poly. Exact cells are preserved. Prompt labels are
hypotheses, not promotions, and no contact sheet substitutes for direct visual
inspection.

Claim ceiling: this atlas may identify repeatable wording sub-basins and
nominate exact reconstruction sources. It cannot establish improved geometry
without matched TRELLIS output and close-orbit inspection.
