## Comparison

Against the broad-plane source, the clean-shape source finishes 25.2s faster, reduces raw faces by 9.5%, final cleaned faces by 61.8%, GLB size by 62.7%, and holes classified too large by 61.5%. More importantly, both cleaned sources visibly avoid the crystalline-fur shredding and conspicuous front-shell tears seen in the original photoreal-source casts.

The clean-shape result has almost the same raw face count as the original photoreal seed-81408 cast (5.06M versus 5.11M) but finishes at 78,657 faces rather than 199,836. Source organization therefore does not simply reduce the decoder's raw occupancy; it materially improves the simplification/cleanup outcome and visible shell.

The close-orbit smoke sharpens the route distinction. The broad-plane source preserves more natural identity but leaves TRELLIS little explicit grammar for the unseen rear fur, where it resolves to a generic smooth cap. The feature-animation source supplies broad, discrete, three-dimensional part boundaries around the full silhouette. TRELLIS completes those parts into an unexpectedly coherent stylized object, including the unseen rear ruff. The corresponding cleanup counts also improve: 22,599 non-manifold faces removed rather than 85,217, and 854 too-large holes rather than 2,221. The operator-visible GTAO result is consistent with that cleaner surface, though it is not an independent manifold test.

## Claim

For this exact frontal horned-head source family and local no-cascade MLX route, source surface frequency and part organization are material causes of the prior roughness. Broad-plane cleanup improves visible shell continuity; explicit stylized feature construction additionally produces coherent unseen-side completion, reduces export burden and repair count, and survives GTAO-enabled operator orbit. The local route still emits millions of raw faces, hundreds of too-large holes, and non-production geometry. This result promotes the feature-animation cast as a reusable geometry-first stylized asset and the broad-plane cast as an identity-bearing bounded-view asset. It does not establish production topology or parity with reference CUDA TRELLIS.
