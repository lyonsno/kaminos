# Cross-Source Matrix Review

All sixteen outputs were inspected directly at full resolution against their row source. The fixed comparison held the source-neutral preservation clause, style clause, requested route, and seed constant while varying only source bytes. This establishes directional repeatability at seed `81439`; it does not establish seed stability or spatial reconstruction quality.

## Verdict

The operator-preferred pair are both real, reusable basins, but they belong at different places in an authoring stack.

- `06-feature-animation-sculptural` is the strongest cross-source construction transform. Every row acquires the same rounded, attached shingle or petal vocabulary while retaining the large anatomical envelope, gaze, camera, and major silhouette. This is the preferred candidate for a second-seed stability check and later reconstruction assay.
- `21-soft-cel-painted-resin` is a strong user-final character-authoring and rendering transform. It consistently produces polished, legible character busts, but it is not identity-neutral: the bear drifts toward a canid, and both feline alternates acquire orange tiger markings. Use it when authored reinterpretation is desired, not when source identity must remain exact.
- `17-beveled-connected-planes` is the most identity-conservative structural control. It repeatedly preserves the source head and camera while adding a legible connected-planar overlay. This is the cleanest candidate when the desired intermediate is simplified geometric organization rather than final style.
- `16-matte-resin-gallery-sculpture` reliably converts fur into tangible sculptural surface while preserving anatomy. It also amplifies markings on both feline alternates, and the canonical result has weak hollow-looking eyes. It is useful as a physical-finish basin, with a weaker identity contract than connected planes.

## Promotion

The operator preferred `21-soft-cel-painted-resin` and `06-feature-animation-sculptural`. Promote both with explicit contracts: soft-cel as an expressive final authoring style, feature-animation as a repeatable surface-construction style. Keep connected planes as the identity-preserving structural reference.

The cheapest next assay is a second fixed seed on the eight preferred cells. If the same identity pressure and shingle construction recur, the result supports exposing these as named authoring controls rather than treating their wording as primitive generator language.
