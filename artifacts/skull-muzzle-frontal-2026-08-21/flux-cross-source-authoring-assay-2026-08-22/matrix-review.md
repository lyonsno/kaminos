# Cross-Source Matrix Review

All sixteen seed-`81439` outputs and all eight operator-preferred seed-`81440` outputs were inspected directly at full resolution against their row source. The first comparison held the source-neutral preservation clause, style clause, requested route, and seed constant while varying only source bytes. The second held all of those coordinates and source bytes fixed while varying only the generation seed. This establishes directional cross-source repeatability and two-seed macro-behavior evidence; it does not establish universal prompt behavior or spatial reconstruction quality.

## Verdict

The operator-preferred pair are both real, reusable basins, but they belong at different places in an authoring stack.

- `06-feature-animation-sculptural` is the strongest cross-source construction transform. At both seeds, every row acquires the same rounded, attached shingle or petal vocabulary while retaining the large anatomical envelope, camera, and major silhouette. Palette, eye completion, and finish are not stable: seed `81440` often converges on monochrome clay with blank eyes. Promote the macro construction, not the exact finish.
- `21-soft-cel-painted-resin` is a strong user-final character-authoring and rendering transform, not a locked style token. Both seeds consistently produce polished, legible character busts, but exact graphics, markings, terminators, costume, and species fidelity vary. The canonical seed-`81440` output invents a suit-and-tie bust; the bear becomes more bear-like than seed `81439`; the feline rows range from explicit tiger graphics to smooth simplified forms. Use it when authored reinterpretation is desired, not when source identity or exact style must remain fixed.
- `17-beveled-connected-planes` is the most identity-conservative structural control. It repeatedly preserves the source head and camera while adding a legible connected-planar overlay. This is the cleanest candidate when the desired intermediate is simplified geometric organization rather than final style.
- `16-matte-resin-gallery-sculpture` reliably converts fur into tangible sculptural surface while preserving anatomy. It also amplifies markings on both feline alternates, and the canonical result has weak hollow-looking eyes. It is useful as a physical-finish basin, with a weaker identity contract than connected planes.

## Promotion

The operator preferred `21-soft-cel-painted-resin` and `06-feature-animation-sculptural`. Promote both with explicit contracts: soft-cel as an expressive final authoring family with substantial stochastic latitude, feature-animation as a repeatable macro surface-construction style with a seed-variable finish. Keep connected planes as the identity-preserving structural reference.

The two-seed result supports exposing these as named authoring controls rather than treating their wording as primitive generator language. The next composition should separate controls accordingly: a macro-construction selector for feature-animation, and a higher-variance authored-look selector for soft-cel whose preview is the authoritative result rather than a promise of exact deterministic styling.
