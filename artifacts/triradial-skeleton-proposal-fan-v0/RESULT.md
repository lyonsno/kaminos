# Tri-radial Skeleton Proposal Fan V0

## Campaign state

The authored three-limb source has exposed a clean stage boundary, but it has not yet produced an admitted envelope-authoring proposal. FLUX can recover exact three-support topology from the support-legible oblique projection, or it can produce a happy familiar character, but the current text-only route did not compose those properties in the same output. Trellis did not repair that gap.

The earlier claim that a happy three-limbed completion survived the full route is superseded by this campaign. The selected happy FLUX proposal was initially misread as three-support; close inspection resolves five distinct articulated leg chains. Its Trellis cast contains additional crossed and duplicated appendage structure across six orbit views.

## What the fan established

The recovery campaign ran 36 source/prompt/seed cells: 24 cells across frontal and support-legible oblique projections, followed by 6 familiar-biological-prior cells and 6 direct tissue-wrapping cells.

- The frontal projection did not yield a useful exact-tripod biological completion.
- The oblique projection materially improved topology legibility. `A living tripodal organism.` recovered exactly three continuous organic supports in one seed. That output was psychologically hostile and structurally unsuitable as an envelope-authoring reference, so it remains an internal boundary result.
- `Complete this skeleton ...` commonly preserved skeletal material literally instead of completing a body around it.
- Familiar mammal and feline priors produced happy conventional animals, but overwrote or merely overlaid the authored three-support relation.
- Direct muscle, skin, and fur prompts wrapped exposed bones literally or placed a conventional mammal inside the unchanged skeleton. They did not infer the missing low-frequency body mass.

The strongest current claim is therefore relational: source projection matters enough to expose the intended topology, and concise prompts can either preserve unfamiliar topology or invoke a friendly biological basin. Text alone has not bridged those two basins.

## Reconstruction result

The happy `oblique-bone / p14 / seed 80413` cell was reconstructed through `trellis2mlx_fast` as a topology probe. The effective route completed and the resulting textured GLB loaded through Kaminos's registered mesh route. A source-hash-bound six-view orbit then falsified topology preservation.

The FLUX proposal already contains five articulated leg chains rather than three. Trellis reconstructs the character coherently at the level of affect, head, body, and material, but rearranges the surplus lower structures into crossed and duplicated appendages. This is not a three-support cast and cannot serve as the low-frequency envelope target.

## Program consequence

The next high-leverage arm is the operator-proposed one: author a low-frequency body envelope around a tripodal structure that is already visually and mechanically legible, then use the generator to elaborate that envelope. More prompt synonyms against the naked skeleton are now lower leverage. The prompt fan has already shown why: a naked unfamiliar skeleton asks the model to solve topology, body mass, material, anatomy, and affect simultaneously, and its priors split those jobs rather than composing them.

The negative result does not reject the tripod program. It identifies the missing intervention. The authored envelope should reduce the completion problem from inventing a body plan to elaborating a body plan, while preserving the three-support relation as visible exterior mass rather than asking either FLUX or Trellis to infer it from exposed bones.

## Effective routes

FLUX used `mflux_flux2_edit_promptfile` with `flux2-klein-9b`, Q4, `512 x 512`, 8 steps, guidance `1.0`, and matched seeds `80301`, `80302`, and `80413`.

The topology probe used `trellis2mlx_fast`, seed `80413`, resolution `512`, 6 steps, no cascade, target 200,000 faces, texture size 1024, and simplify-first. One preliminary command failed before model execution because its working directory pointed at Kaminos instead of the Trellis route; the corrected command completed. The failed command contributes no model evidence.
