# Direct three-legged creature basin campaign

## Campaign state

The first 24 text-to-image cells have settled a useful separation. Neutral `three-legged creature` language can produce exact three-leg organization, but the admitted topology cells are not positively aesthetic. Friendly, furry, animal, pet, and fantasy-animal language reliably produces happy and envelope-authorable bodies, but restores ordinary biped or quadruped topology. No text-only cell clears both gates.

This is not a generator-wide failure. It localizes the conflict to composition between the unconventional support count and the strong happy-organism prior under concise text-only prompting.

## What changed

- Exact three-leg topology is available from text alone: 2 of 24 cells.
- Positive aesthetic organization is available from text alone: 15 of 24 cells.
- The conjunction did not occur: 0 of 24 cells.
- Anatomical placement wording expanded appendage count through an insectoid prior instead of constraining support placement.
- `animal`, `furry`, `pet`, and `fantasy animal` suppressed the insectoid basin but overrode the requested third leg.

## Local edit result

The bounded local image-edit assay is settled. Ten edits covered two happy four-legged parents, left- and right-rear-leg deletion, two seeds, and both imperative and declarative target-state wording. All ten outputs remained positively aesthetic and preserved the parent identity. All ten also retained four distinct legs. No cell cleared the topology gate, so no output was submitted to Trellis.

The consistency matters. This route is capable of preserving a happy organism basin across an edit, but concise semantic language is not carrying support cardinality against the ordinary-animal prior. More wording would now be a synonym search rather than a new mechanism.

## Current continuation

The next useful branch is spatial control: an authored three-leg envelope or explicit mask that makes the missing support true in the conditioning image before asking the generator to elaborate appearance. The existing operator-authored three-leg skeleton remains useful as structural substrate, but naked-skeleton completion and unmasked semantic editing are both settled as weak routes for obtaining the happy source image.

## Claim ceiling

The text-only evidence establishes route-specific 2D basin behavior under FLUX.2 Klein 9B, four-bit quantization, 1024 square output, four steps, guidance 1.0, and the recorded seeds. The edit evidence establishes local invariance under the same model at eight steps and the recorded edit seeds. Neither phase establishes 3D topology or Trellis reconstruction fidelity.
