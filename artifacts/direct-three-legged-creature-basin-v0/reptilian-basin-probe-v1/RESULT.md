# Exact-three basin probe

## Campaign state

The exact-three text-to-image basin is fertile enough to use as a proposal source. It is not a stable reptilian style basin, and it is not yet a 3D result.

The neutral prompt produced exact-three figures in two of three fresh seeds. Including historical cells `d01-seed80301`, `d01-seed80302`, and `d01-seed80413`, the observed exact-three rate is three of six. The machine-checkable source binding lives in `classification.json` and `../text-only-classification.json`. This rejects the hypothesis that seed 80301 was merely a unique lucky conjunction.

The fixed-seed arm also found one useful steering phrase: `three legs diverging visibly from one pelvis`. At seed 80301, that relational wording preserved the reptilian phenotype and yielded three explicit load-bearing legs. Generic reptilian wording produced four legs, while `friendly` restored familiar biped structure. Explicit `pelvis` wording without the divergence relation literalized the anatomy and was counterproductive.

## What changed

- Exact-three proposal generation no longer needs to begin from a hostile skeletal render. A short neutral text prompt has a meaningful hit rate.
- Relational support wording is more useful than naming an anatomical region. The generator responds to `diverging visibly` as geometry, while bare `pelvis` pressure becomes semantic surface content.
- Aesthetic pressure and topology pressure are not additive in this sample. `Friendly` improved affect but erased the third leg.
- The strongest next source is `control-seed80503`: it has three unambiguous legs, no arms, a clean silhouette, and the lowest envelope-authoring ambiguity.

## Recommended next experiment

After operator inspection, advance `control-seed80503` first to Trellis if its aesthetic and authoring value hold up; preserve `p05-seed80301` and `control-seed80502` as comparison candidates. The next claim is narrow: whether Trellis can preserve an exact-three proposal clearly enough for authored-envelope registration. No result here establishes 3D topology, riggability, or production anatomy.
