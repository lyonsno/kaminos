## Mechanism redirect

attachment-zero displacement cannot change endpoint penetration. The next
assay is a bounded endpoint-radius taper with interior cross-section
compensation. It must keep attachment positions, source identity, target
volume, requested/effective derivation identity, and the reviewed condition
order exact; it may only redistribute cross-section along each centerline.

The falsifier is direct: if a modest taper cannot clear immutable preflight,
or if compensation creates a visually swollen or pinched belly, endpoint
cross-section policy is not the missing degree of freedom. If it opens
preflight, Packer resumes the previously planned axial/azimuthal mechanism
