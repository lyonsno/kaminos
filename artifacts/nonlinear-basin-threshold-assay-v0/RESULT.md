# Density pass result

## Campaign state

Automatic decimation is substantially less damaging than expected. With the prompt, seed, route, framing, and authored envelope held fixed, FLUX remains in essentially the same happy fur-bearing organism basin from the 2,070-triangle positive control through the 615-triangle cell. The first obvious loss is local rather than global: at 307 triangles, cranial cues, distal supports, and head-to-neck articulation become generic, while the quadruped body plan still survives.

This moves the research program. Polygon density is not the leading explanation for preservation across the useful range of this asset. Anatomical organization is now the more informative variable to attack.

## What became more confident

- Automatic decimation can produce aesthetically coherent conditioning plates at 1,539, 1,025, and 615 triangles without forcing FLUX into a different organism basin.
- The route preserves the authored mass distribution, stance, neck, head direction, and tail organization even after roughly 70% of the evaluated triangles are removed.
- The density ladder is suitable as a precursor and control surface; operator authorship is not needed merely to create lower-resolution cells.

## What remains uncertain

The 615- and 307-triangle cells are not pure density interventions. At those levels, automatic collapse also starts damaging anatomical organization. The pass therefore cannot establish a universal polygon threshold or tell us whether the 307-triangle break was caused by count, organization, or their interaction.

SSIM against the positive-control output declines smoothly from 0.983205 at 1,539 triangles to 0.868015 at 307 triangles. That metric supports continuity, but it is not an admission criterion and does not replace morphology inspection.

## Next experiment

Run two operator-authored, camera-identical, same-density structure degradations. The first should remove specific shoulder, pelvic, and support-chain organization while remaining cleanly organismal. The second should weaken those relationships further without introducing cavities, repetitions, corruption cues, or other accidental semantic priors. Keep topology and polygon budget comparable to the positive control so the independent variable is structural quality rather than resolution.

Only after those two independent axes are legible should the campaign spend an interaction cell at `S1 × r050`.
