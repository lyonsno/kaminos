# Surface-connected part proposals

`tools/registration_surface.py --manifest /absolute/path/to/run.json` compares the saved analytical-carrier fit with independent claims grown over a retained TRELLIS mesh. It is an offline experiment; no browser or model dependency is added. Install the Python packages in `requirements.txt` in a private environment. Full meshes belong in the shared compute queue.

The manifest chooses an empty `output_dir`; `inputs` contains `source`, `fit`, `target`, `raw_mesh`, and `raw_metadata`, each with an absolute `path` and expected `sha256`. These are the analytical `.kaminos.json`, saved asset registration, final GLB, original `mesh_raw.npz`, and `mesh_raw.json`. The target must be in its native exported frame. `parameters` supplies positive scene-unit `seed_radius`, `seed_margin`, `reach`, and `ambiguity`; `transfer_tolerance` bounds the distance from each final triangle centroid to its nearest raw vertex. Keep the manifest with the outputs.

Each role starts from vertices close to its analytical solids and sufficiently farther from the other roles. Its claim spreads along actual triangle edges, with a cost for leaving its analytical envelope. Near ties remain multiply claimed. Unreachable or distant surfaces remain unclaimed. This is a geometric proposal, not a semantic confidence score or a recovered mechanical joint. The original TRELLIS export convention is `(x,y,z) -> (x,z,-y)`.

`raw-claims.npz` preserves all vertex distances, seeds, path costs and claims. `target-claims.npz` preserves every final triangle's raw correspondence, transfer distance, support flag, and baseline/candidate bitsets. `baseline.glb` and `candidate.glb` preserve all final triangles: cyan base, gold boom, purple barrel, pink rod, orange unclaimed, magenta multiply claimed. Role GLBs retain the final mesh's materials and UVs, and include overlapping triangles in each claimant. They are open surface proposals; no hidden geometry or watertight caps are fabricated. Load these through the existing Kaminos asset/scene flow to use normal selection, visibility, transforms and saving.

`report.json` records input hashes, parameters, mesh sizes, correspondence distances and output hashes. A failure after manifest admission records its phase and last trustworthy evidence. Existing nonempty output directories are refused. Tiny deterministic tests run with `npm test -- --only tests/registration-surface.py`; they cover disconnected residuals, overlaps, observed export coordinates, complete triangle preservation, GLB reloading, wrong input identity and empty geometry.
