# Liquid-Driven Goin Pile

`hand-goin-pile` composes the existing browser hand and finger-packet law with a physical pile. `mls-juice` accepts the same `goinPile` setup for deterministic nozzle diagnosis. This is a provisional flat-support interaction, not the Hill or Horde's possession rules.

The pile uses Cannon ES 0.20.0, matching Horde's existing goin engine and Y-axis cylinder convention. Twenty-four coins begin in four six-coin stacks. Mass, radius, thickness, count, collision side count and coupling steps are explicit setup parameters. The solver reports their effective values and every body's pose, linear/angular velocity, contact count and accumulated liquid impulse. Gold beveled render meshes are visual proxies for the physical cylinders.

After each MLS G2P step, particles encountering a moving cylinder receive a normal contact impulse using relative surface velocity and the particle/body effective mass. The opposite impulse and its moment about the body center are accumulated on the GPU. World particle mass is explicitly 0.001 by default; this is a gameplay mass scale, not a calibrated water-density claim. The fluid's APIC constitutive model is otherwise unchanged. Tangential liquid friction, buoyancy/pressure integration, arbitrary scene collision and dynamic Hill support are not implemented.

At `couplingSteps: 1`, each liquid substep returns its impulses before the next substep; Cannon integrates them with gravity and body/body/floor contact. At larger values, the GPU uses constant-velocity extrapolated body poses for that explicit interval, retaining a separate impulse slice for every substep. Cannon then consumes those slices in order, with its fixed timestep unchanged. This buffered candidate does not have immediate two-way feedback inside the interval. Its contact fidelity and stability must be compared with the exact-step reference; a faster return does not establish equivalence. The fluid collider is an analytic cylinder; Cannon's faceted cylinder resolution is independently reported.

Readback is asynchronous and hand presentation does not await it. All wall-clock debt remains visible. `couplingTiming` separates CPU wall time waiting for GPU/readback from CPU rigid-body stepping; neither is a GPU timestamp measurement. Pausing stops further dispatch after the in-flight transaction, and disposal stops its continuation. Reset pile rebuilds water and bodies through the existing fluid-session lifecycle without restarting the camera.

Use `npm test -- --only tests/mls-goin-pile.mjs,tests/mls-pending-pause.mjs,tests/mls-runway.mjs`. `tools/mls-juice-witness.mjs` retains source-bound setup, rendered motion, state chronology, complete native particle snapshot and failure report. Camera composition uses the existing hand witness and retains its actual source frames and predictions. The original runway remains a separate comparison route.
